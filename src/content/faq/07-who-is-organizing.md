question: Who is organizing Ghana Day in Tampa Bay 2026?
order: 7
---

Ghana Day in Tampa Bay 2026 is presented by the Ghanaian Association of Tampa Bay. The maiden edition celebrates and preserves Ghanaian culture, promotes diversity and multicultural engagement, encourages cultural exchange, and showcases Ghanaian entrepreneurship, arts, and traditions. Ghanaian and African diaspora associations, businesses, and community groups from across Florida and neighboring states are warmly invited to attend and participate.
